# HSK3-L18 课堂图片生成提示词

> 生成日期：2026-11-06
> 用途：记录阶段3正式课程使用的8张教学扩展图片及最终提示词要点。

## 统一视觉要求

Create a photorealistic natural lifestyle photograph for an HSK 3 Chinese classroom lesson, horizontal 16:9 composition suitable for classroom projection. Keep all faces, hands and key teaching objects natural and clearly visible. Use a culturally respectful contemporary setting. No collage, no split screen, no readable text, no logos, no brands and no watermark.

## 1. 飞机上的春节话题

Scene: a young international female student and a young Chinese male friend sit next to each other on a commercial airplane and talk happily about spending Spring Festival in China. Include travel bags and a few tasteful red Spring Festival objects near the luggage so the festival topic is visually clear. Leave safe open space around the two speakers.

输出：`photo-text-1-plane-festival-talk.png`

## 2. 一家人包饺子看晚会

Scene: a warm Chinese family home on Spring Festival Eve. A middle-aged couple, their young adult son and his international female friend make jiaozi together around a dining table while a colourful but text-free gala plays on the television. Show wrappers, filling, finished dumplings and a wall clock close to midnight.

输出：`photo-text-1-family-dumplings-gala.png`

## 3. 第一次见面送礼物

Scene: at the entrance of a Chinese family home during Spring Festival, a young international female guest politely offers a wrapped red gift to a smiling middle-aged Chinese couple. Their young adult son introduces them. Show welcoming open-hand gestures, simple red decorations and a prepared family table in the background.

输出：`photo-text-2-new-year-greeting-gift.png`

## 4. 看视频学包饺子

Scene: a multigenerational family and a young international female guest make jiaozi around a dining table. A phone propped on the table shows a close-up dumpling-folding tutorial without any interface text. Include flour, wrappers, filling, finished dumplings, drinks and one clear bottle of mineral water.

输出：`photo-text-2-learning-dumplings-video.png`

## 5. 机场旧照片（质检后重做版）

Scene: at home, a middle-aged Chinese mother holds one slightly aged printed family photograph in both hands while showing it to a young international female guest seated beside her. The printed photograph is clearly visible and shows the mother, father, and their young adult son standing together at a modern Beijing airport before the son leaves to study abroad, with one suitcase and a distant control tower. The mother looks nostalgic but warm; the guest listens with interest. Make the old airport photograph the clear focal point, while the present-day home background is softly blurred. No duplicate live airport scene.

输出：`photo-text-3-airport-family-photo.png`

## 6. 妈妈和儿子视频

Scene: a middle-aged Chinese mother sits at a home table and smiles during a video call with her adult son studying abroad. Add a graduation cap on books, a simple calendar with several marked days, a framed family photo and a warm evening home atmosphere. The tablet screen clearly shows the son but no interface text.

输出：`photo-text-3-mother-video-call.png`

## 7. 相册里的成长故事

Scene: a middle-aged Chinese couple and a young international female guest sit together on a sofa looking at a large family photo album. The open album clearly shows the same boy at three life stages: a baby, a schoolchild and a high-school student studying. The mother points to the baby photo while everyone shares warm memories.

输出：`photo-text-4-life-photo-album.png`

## 8. 从学习到目标

Scene: a focused Chinese high-school student studies at a desk in daylight. Around him are a study checklist, a calendar with repeated practice days, books, a small photo of his earlier school years and a university image tucked into an envelope. Show steady daily effort and a future study-abroad goal through real objects, not an infographic.

输出：`photo-text-4-study-goal-timeline.png`
