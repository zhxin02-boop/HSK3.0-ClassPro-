ClassPro 用智能连接课前发现、课中互动与课后反馈：让教师看见学习如何发生，据此调整教学；让学生看见自己如何成长，知道下一步去哪里。

ClassPro connects discovery before class, interaction during class, and feedback after class—helping teachers see how learning happens and helping learners see their growth and next step.
